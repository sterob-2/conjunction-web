Privacy Policy

Version 1.3 · Last updated 31 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Conjunction (the “app”), an Android game distributed through Google Play. It is written for the version of the app that is available today, and it is dated above so you can tell when it last changed.

The short version

Your progress is stored on your device. Two things leave it only if you tick them: a copy of your heroes in the cloud, and gameplay statistics — moments of play such as a fight won or a level reached, with no name and nothing you typed. Both start unticked; the app asks you once and sends nothing unless you tick one, and you can change either at any time under Settings → Gameplay → Privacy. There is no advertising and no tracking across other apps. A feedback report reaches us only when you send it yourself.

What the app stores, and where

Your save game — character, progress, inventory, claimed fields and settings — is written to the app’s private storage area on your device. While cloud save is on, a copy of it is kept in the cloud as described below.

Next to each hero’s save the app keeps a play diary: one line for each moment of play — the app was started or brought back, a fight began or ended, a field was cleared for the first time, the hero died, a field’s entry check turned the hero away, the hero switched equipment set, a hunt was collected, a level was reached. A line holds game values only, such as the field, the hero’s level, the outcome, how long it took and what was found, together with a random identifier for the hero and one for the current app session, the app and game-data version, whether the game’s test options were used, and the time. It holds no name, no device identifier and nothing you typed. The app keeps this diary only while you share gameplay statistics, and its lines are the statistics the app sends.

The two choices, Back up my heroes in the cloud and Share gameplay statistics, are under Settings → Gameplay → Privacy. Unticking the statistics deletes the diary of every hero on the device and nothing new is kept until you tick them again; unticking cloud save stops new cloud copies.

Uninstalling the app removes everything on the device. It does not delete the cloud copy of your heroes or the statistics already sent; see Your rights for how to have them deleted.

Before anything is sent

The first time you start the app — or, if you played an earlier version, the next time you open the world map — it shows a notice that explains cloud save and gameplay statistics, with a box for each and a Continue button. Both boxes start unticked. Until you tick one, the app sends nothing, connects to no service and creates no identifier for the services below; if you continue without ticking, that stays so until you tick a choice in Settings yourself. The app stores only your answer on the device, so it does not ask again.

Cloud save

So that a lost or new phone does not mean a lost hero, the app keeps a copy of each hero’s save in Unity Gaming Services Cloud Save, a service of Unity Technologies that stores it on our behalf under a data processing agreement. The copy is the whole save: the hero’s name as you chose it, level, equipment, bag, gold, story progress and the hero’s random identifier. It is sent about twenty seconds after the game saves, and only while you have ticked cloud save. The copy is stored under a random player identifier the service gives the app when it first connects; the app keeps that identifier on your device.

You can add a sign-in with Google (Google Play Games on Android). It links your Google Play Games account to that player identifier, so you can find your heroes again on a new phone; Google then learns that you use the app, under Google’s own terms as an independent controller. The sign-in is never required.

Legal basis: providing the cloud save you asked for by ticking it, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; the player identifier on your device is strictly necessary for that service (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The copy is kept until you delete your cloud account in the app under Settings → Gameplay → Account, or by writing to us. Unticking cloud save stops new copies but does not delete the stored one.

Usage statistics

To understand how the game is played and where players get stuck, the app sends the lines of the play diary to Unity Gaming Services Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies that processes them on our behalf under a data processing agreement. These are the nine moments of play the diary records: app started or brought back, fight started, fight ended, field cleared for the first time, hero died, entry check failed, equipment set switched, hunt collected and level reached — nothing else of what you do in the game is sent.

To tell one installation from another, the service gives the app a random installation identifier, stored on your device; once the app has signed in for the cloud save, the random player identifier from above is attached as well. Together with the random hero and session identifiers in the diary, this makes the statistics pseudonymous. We do not use them to identify you, do not combine them with other data and do not use them for advertising. The service also processes your IP address, the approximate location derived from it, and technical diagnostics and performance data of the app.

Unity documents that it processes Analytics data on servers in Belgium and the Netherlands and keeps a replicated copy in Iowa, United States. Raw events are kept for 13 months; the counts and player profiles derived from them are kept until deletion is requested.

Legal basis: your consent, given by ticking Share gameplay statistics, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which also covers storing the installation identifier on your device (§ 25(1) TDDDG). We use the statistics only to find where the game is too hard or unclear and to balance it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by unticking the statistics in Settings; this does not affect what was sent before. From that moment nothing more is collected, the diary on your device is deleted, and the app asks Unity to delete what it received from this installation — if your phone is offline, it asks again until the request has gone through.

Where the services process your data

Unity Technologies processes cloud copies and statistics as our processor (Art. 28 GDPR). Where data leaves the European Union, Unity’s data processing agreement relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Unity names its storage locations and sub-processors in that agreement. All data the app sends travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Feedback reports

The game has a feedback button for reporting bugs and ideas. When you tap it and send, the app packs one file containing:

  • the text you typed and whether it is a bug or an idea;
  • the app version, your device model and its Android version;
  • the save game of every hero on this device — each character and the name you gave it, progress, inventory, skills and settings, and whether the game’s test options were used;
  • the play diary of every hero on this device, if you share gameplay statistics;
  • the recordings of the fights not sent in an earlier report, which the game keeps on your device so a fight can be traced afterwards, including any note you added to a fight with “Felt wrong?”.

The app does not send this file itself. It saves the file in your device’s Downloads folder and opens Android’s share menu. You choose the app it goes through (for example your e-mail app or Discord) and the recipient; for e-mail the address feedback@conjunctiongame.com is filled in for you. If you cancel, nothing is sent. The copy in Downloads stays until your next report replaces it or you delete it.

We use what you send only to find and fix errors and to balance the game. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in doing that, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We keep a report for as long as we are working on what it describes, and delete it earlier if you ask. The app you send it through also handles the report, along with whatever it carries with it, such as your e-mail address or Discord name, under its own terms. If you post a report in a channel that other people can read, they can see it too.

Permissions the app requests

  • Network access — for the cloud save and the gameplay statistics while you have ticked them, and for the optional Google sign-in.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback on critical hits. No data is read or transmitted.
  • Notifications — so the game can tell you when a field you set to farm has finished. These notifications are generated on your device by the app itself; no server is involved and no information is sent anywhere.
  • Foreground service — declared by Google Play’s asset delivery, which unpacks the game’s graphics after an install or update. No data is read or transmitted.

The app requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Google Play

The distribution channel is not ours. When you download, install or update the app through Google Play, Google processes data about that — and, if you have enabled it on your device, may share crash diagnostics with developers in aggregated form. This processing is carried out by Google under its own terms, not by us. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the data safety section on the app’s Play Store page.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records server log data such as your IP address when a page is served; this is technically necessary to deliver the page and is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site sets no cookies and runs no tracking or analytics. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts a Google server and your IP address is transmitted in that request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

In practice:

  • Statistics: unticking them in Settings withdraws your consent; the app then asks Unity to delete what it received from this installation. Without the app, write to the contact address above.
  • Cloud copies: delete your cloud account in the app under Settings → Gameplay → Account, or follow the steps on Delete your account. Settings also show your Player ID, which a request by e-mail names.
  • Feedback reports: to get a copy of a report, or have it deleted, write to the contact address above. You may object to our use of a report at any time, and we will then delete it.

We answer every request within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not designed for them. A parent or guardian can untick both choices, delete the cloud account in the app, or write to the contact address above, and we will delete what we hold.

Changes to this policy

The app is in active development. Online leaderboards and advertising are not part of the app today. If either is added, or the app starts to send anything not described here, this policy will be updated before the corresponding version is released, the version number and date above will change, and the data safety declaration on the Play Store page will be updated to match.
