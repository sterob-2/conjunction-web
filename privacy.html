Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 12 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Conjunction (the “app”), an Android game distributed through Google Play. It is written for the version of the app that is available today, and it is dated above so you can tell when it last changed.

The short version

The app collects nothing. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting of its own and no network communication whatsoever. Your progress is stored on your device and nowhere else. Nothing you do in the game reaches us or any third party.

What the app stores, and where

Your save game — character, progress, inventory, claimed fields and settings — is written to the app’s private storage area on your device. It never leaves the device. We have no copy of it and no way to read it.

Uninstalling the app removes this data. There is nothing further you need to do and nothing for us to delete, because we never had it.

Permissions the app requests

  • Vibration — for haptic feedback on critical hits. No data is read or transmitted.
  • Notifications — so the game can tell you when a field you set to farm has finished. These notifications are generated on your device by the app itself; no server is involved and no information is sent anywhere.

The app requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Google Play

The app itself sends nothing to us, but the distribution channel is not ours. When you download, install or update the app through Google Play, Google processes data about that — and, if you have enabled it on your device, may share crash diagnostics with developers in aggregated form. This processing is carried out by Google under its own terms, not by us. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the data safety section on the app’s Play Store page.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records server log data such as your IP address when a page is served; this is technically necessary to deliver the page and is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site sets no cookies and runs no tracking or analytics. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts a Google server and your IP address is transmitted in that request.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), and you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77).

In practice there is nothing here for these rights to act on: we hold no data about you. If that changes, this policy changes with it, and the version and date at the top will say so.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not designed for them. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

The app is in active development. Features that would process data — online leaderboards, advertising or usage statistics — are not part of the app today. If any of them is added, this policy will be updated before the corresponding version is released, the version number and date above will change, and the data safety declaration on the Play Store page will be updated to match.
