Sign in

Your Conjunction account on the website

Sign in with the Google account you linked to your heroes in the game. The website only reads your account; it changes nothing in it and never makes a new one.

Not linked yet? In the game, open Settings, then Gameplay, then Account, and tap Link with Google. Then sign in here with the same Google account.

Your browser contacts Google only when you tap Sign in with Google. What signing in sends and keeps, and for how long, is in the privacy policy.
