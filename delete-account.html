Delete your account

Conjunction, the dark fantasy RPG for Android by Robin von dem Knesebeck

Conjunction keeps a copy of your heroes in the cloud while Cloud save and gameplay statistics is on, under a cloud account the game makes for you. You can delete that account and every copy kept under it at any time, in the game or by writing to us.

In the game

  1. Open Settings and the Gameplay tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

The account and the cloud copies of all your heroes are deleted at once, and cloud save and gameplay statistics are unticked on that phone. The heroes on the phone itself stay; you delete them on the title screen or by uninstalling the game.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game, write to info@conjunctiongame.com with the subject Delete my Conjunction account and the Player ID shown under Settings → Gameplay → Account.

We delete the account and its cloud copies within 30 days and confirm by reply. Without the Player ID we cannot tell which account is yours: an account is known to us only by that random id.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: the cloud account with its random Player ID, and the cloud copy of every hero kept under it (the whole save: name, level, equipment, bag, gold and story progress).
  • Not kept by us: nothing of the account remains in the cloud save once it is deleted.
  • Gameplay statistics sent while you shared them carry no name and are handled as the privacy policy describes; ask in the same e-mail and we request their deletion too.
