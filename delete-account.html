Delete your account

Conjunction, the dark fantasy RPG for Android by Robin von dem Knesebeck

Conjunction keeps a copy of your heroes in the cloud while Cloud save and gameplay statistics is on, under a cloud account the game makes for you. You can delete that account and every copy kept under it at any time, in the game or by writing to us.

In the game

  1. Open Settings and the Gameplay tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

The account and the cloud copies of all your heroes are deleted at once, and cloud save switches off on that phone. The heroes on the phone itself stay; you delete them on the title screen or by uninstalling the game.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game, write to info@conjunctiongame.com with the subject Delete my Conjunction account and one of these:

  • the Player ID shown under Settings → Gameplay → Account, or
  • the Google Play Games account you signed in to the game with.

We delete the account and its cloud copies within 30 days and confirm by reply. Without one of the two we cannot tell which account is yours: an account that never signed in with Google is known to us only by its random Player ID.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: the cloud account with its random Player ID and its link to Google Play Games, and the cloud copy of every hero kept under it (the whole save: name, level, equipment, bag, gold and story progress).
  • Not kept by us: nothing of the account remains in the cloud save once it is deleted.
  • Gameplay statistics sent while the switch was on carry no name and are handled as the privacy policy describes; ask in the same e-mail and we request their deletion too.

Your Google account itself and what Google keeps about your use of Google Play Games are Google's; manage them in your Google account.
